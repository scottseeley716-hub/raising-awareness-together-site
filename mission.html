Why this exists

From broken to becoming

Nobody here arrived polished. We arrived tired, scammed out, lost, and at the end of it all, a little bit angry and frustrated, because we knew there was more out of life than this.

After

I built this because I was tired of watching good people suffer, get scammed, and struggle through life simply because they didn't have the knowledge to change their situation.

If this is you, you belong here. Welcome to the family.

No matter what, no matter who, or where you come from, Raising Awareness Together welcomes you to the family. We take helping others very seriously, so come on in, and maybe you can elevate your life to vibrate at your highest potential.

If you see something we could add, we would love your knowledge through experience.
