Before you ask

Plain answers

Are the healing programs really free?

Yes. All seven are free to work through, with no payment step and no upsell inside them. The site may ask you to create a free account so it can save your place. That is the only ask.

Why are the money and AI courses paid?

Because they are skills, and skills are the things that raise your income. The free programs help you get steady. The paid courses help you build something once you are. Charging for the first kind would be wrong. Charging for the second kind is how the first kind stays free.

What does the money actually go to?

The free programs, the site, and the time it takes to keep both running. Donations and course sales are the whole budget. There is no investor and no ad money.

Can I get a refund on a course?

The AI course carries a 30-day Results or It's Free guarantee. If something else has gone wrong, write to us and we will work it out. Nobody gets left holding a course they cannot use.

Is this therapy?

No. These are educational programs built from lived experience and published research. They are not therapy, medical care, or a replacement for either. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.

I have a course in me. Where does that go?

Use the form above. Send the rough idea, not the finished thing. If it fits, we shape it into a program and it goes live alongside everything else.
