Refund Policy

Thirty days, no argument, on paid courses. Donations are different.

Last updated 7 November 2026

1. Paid courses: 30 days, no argument

If a paid course is not for you, write to us within 30 days of your purchase and we will refund it. You do not have to explain yourself, and you do not have to have finished it. This is the "Results or It's Free" promise the course pages mention, kept simply.

What we ask: that you used the material, rather than copied it out and asked for the money back. Where a refund is clearly being used to sample a course and keep the files, we may decline, and we will say so plainly.

2. Free worlds

Nothing is charged for the free programs, so there is nothing to refund. If you see a charge from us you did not expect, tell us and we will find it.

3. Donations

Donations fund free access for people who cannot pay, and they are not refundable as a rule. Two exceptions: a charge that was clearly an error, such as a double tap, and a charge you did not authorise. Email us and we will reverse it.

4. How to ask

Email scottseeley716@gmail.com with:

  • the email address you used at checkout,
  • roughly when you bought, and which course,
  • one line saying you want a refund.

That is all. You will get a reply from a person, not a form letter.

5. How long it takes

We approve refunds within two business days. The money lands back on the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank or card. Klarna and PayPal instalments are reversed through them, on their timeline.

6. Access after a refund

When a course is refunded, access to it ends. Anything you downloaded, please delete.

7. If something went wrong with the payment itself

If a charge looks wrong, or you are not sure what it was for, write to us before filing a chargeback. It is almost always faster: we can see the order and reverse it the same day. Chargebacks take weeks and cost us a fee, which is money that does not go to free access.
