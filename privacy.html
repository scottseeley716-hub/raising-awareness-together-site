Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how to get it removed.

Last updated 7 November 2026

1. The short version

We collect as little as we can get away with. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising profiles on you, and card numbers never touch our systems. The rest of this page is the detail.

2. What we collect

  • What you give us. Your name, email, phone, and whatever you type into a form: a course question, a creator submission, a message in the free guide, a note with a donation.
  • What the store collects. When you buy, our store provider handles the order and the payment. We receive your name, email, what you bought, and the amount. We do not receive or store your card number; that goes straight to the payment processor.
  • What the site records automatically. Pages visited, approximate location from your IP address, device and browser type, and referring link. This is what any website logs.
  • Small things stored in your browser. Local storage and cookies that keep the site working: remembering a dismissed banner, keeping a form's state while you type.

3. Why we use it

  • To deliver what you bought or asked for, and to reply to you.
  • To send the free guide or a course email, when you asked for it.
  • To fix what is broken and understand what is worth building next.
  • To keep the site and its payments safe, and to meet tax and accounting duties.

4. Who else sees it

Only the providers that make the site run, and only for their part: our store and payment providers (Wix, PayPal, Klarna), our email and form delivery, our hosting, and our analytics. They act on our instructions. If the company is ever sold or merged, your information would move with it, and you would hear about it.

We do not sell your information. We share it outside that list only if the law requires it, or to stop someone who is harming other people here.

5. How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Emails and form submissions are kept while the conversation is useful, then cleared. Analytics is kept in aggregate. You can ask us to delete what we hold about you at any time; see below.

6. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time with the link at the bottom of any email, or by writing to us.
  • Access or delete what we hold by emailing scottseeley716@gmail.com. We will confirm within 30 days.
  • Decline cookies through your browser settings. The free worlds still read fine; some conveniences may not stick.
  • If you are in a region with extra rights, such as the EU or UK, you have the rights those laws give you, including complaint to your local authority.

7. Younger readers

The site is written for adults. If you are under 16, please do not send us your information or make a purchase. Crisis support for young people is available through 988 and local lines.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted, access is limited to the people who need it, and payments are processed by providers whose business is handling money safely. No system is perfect; if something ever goes wrong, we will tell the people affected.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and, for anything significant, we will email the people on our list.
