Who we are looking for

Not credentials. Proof you know it.

We are not checking resumes for the right names. We are checking whether you can get someone from stuck to unstuck, in steps they can follow on a hard night.

Lived experience

You came through it

Grief, addiction, abuse, debt, the version of a life that had to be rebuilt. You know the way out because you walked it. That is a course nobody else can teach.

Earned skill

A trade, a business, a craft, a system that pays you. Ten years of it in your head, and people asking you the same questions over again. That is a course.

Real expertise

College, certification, licensure, years in a field. The knowledge is real and it helps people make money or get steady. It does not need to be rare, just useful and teachable.

For a healing course

Free to the people who need it

Healing is never sold here, so there is no sale price to split. If your idea is about the nervous system, grief, crisis, anger, abuse, or addiction, it still gets built, and we agree a different arrangement in writing before we start: a build fee paid to you, or a share of the donations your program brings in.

The free programs are the reason this place exists. Building one is not unpaid work, it is just paid a different way.

Before any money moves, it is in writing. Every accepted submission gets a short creator agreement that names the course, the split, how sales are counted, how long the deal runs, and how either side ends it. Nobody is paid on a handshake and nobody is locked in by surprise.

Taxes and paperwork are handled before the first payout. We collect the tax information the law requires from you, and report what you earn the way we are required to. Cross-border payouts get confirmed case by case, because the rules are not the same in every country. We are not going to describe the tax treatment on a web page as if it were advice: the exact structure gets reviewed with an accountant and a business attorney before money moves, and the terms you sign will match it.

What we will not do: sell your idea to someone else, change your split quietly, or use your name and story in marketing without asking you first.

From idea to income

What happens after you hit send

1

You send the rough version

A working title, who it is for, and what someone can do after finishing it. A voice note or a link to a messy doc is enough. It does not need to be organized, that is our job.

2

We read it and reply either way

If it fits, you get a short call and a plan. If it does not, you get a straight answer and the reason, so the next version has a better shot.

3

We shape it together

Your material becomes sections and steps in the order that helps someone most, in plain language, with the outcome stated on the first page.

4

It goes live with the rest

Skill courses sit in the paid vault. Healing programs join the free worlds. Either way it carries your name as the creator.

5

You get paid as it sells

Monthly, through PayPal, with a statement that shows exactly what sold and what your share was.

Straight talk

What makes an idea sellable

Most rejected submissions are not bad ideas. They are too big, or they are about the teacher instead of the student.

One specific outcome

"Get your first credit score above 650" beats "learn about money." Name the before and the after.

A person you can picture

Who is this for, exactly? The single mother working nights, the guy out of work at 52, the kid who never got taught.

Something you have done

You have to have lived it or worked it. A course built from a book report reads like a book report.

Steps, not a philosophy

If it can be taught in five to fifteen steps someone can finish in a week, it is a course. If it needs a year, it is a book.
